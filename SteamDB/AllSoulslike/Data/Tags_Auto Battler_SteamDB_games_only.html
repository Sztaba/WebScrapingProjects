The 'Auto Battler' tag on Steam is applied to games that feature automated or self-resolving combat systems. In these titles, players engage in strategic decision making during preparation phases, such as selecting units, placing them on the battlefield, and allocating resources. The actual clashes between forces are usually handled by pre-defined rules or artificial intelligence, allowing for a more streamlined and tactical experience. These games often cater to social interaction through multiplayer modes or cooperative gameplay, fostering community engagement.
