The 'Looter Shooter' tag on Steam signifies a specific genre of games where players engage in intense, action-packed experiences that involve both shooting and looting. Developers and community members apply this tag to games that emphasize scavenging for resources, weapons, and gear during combat encounters. Looter Shooters often incorporate role-playing elements, character progression, and cooperative multiplayer. Players delve into immersive worlds filled with dangerous foes and the thrill of discovery, making each playthrough unique.
